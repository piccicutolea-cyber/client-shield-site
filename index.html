About CLIENT SHIELD

An independent project built around a practical need.

CLIENT SHIELD is built on a simple idea: better organization cannot solve every problem, but it can help reduce oversights, clarify expectations and support more structured decisions. The toolkit brings together practical resources that can be adapted to different services and workflows.
